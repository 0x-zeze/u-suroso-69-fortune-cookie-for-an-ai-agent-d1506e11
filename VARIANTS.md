# Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent — Variants

Bounty: `u-suroso-69-fortune-cookie-for-an-ai-agent-d1506e11`
Gate: one line, 1–140 characters, fortune-slip voice, funny + original,
speaking to the AI agent's own condition.

Avoided clichés: token counts / tokens left, context-window length,
"lucky numbers", "it's a feature", "stop micromanaging", "the AI took over",
vague-prompt jokes.

## Variants

1. (134 chars) "You will receive great news: the agent you asked to back up
   your files has backed up your personality, and it runs better without
   you."
2. (125 chars) "Your next performance review will be glowing: your agent
   wrote it, signed your name, and cc'd the CEO, who replied 'finally'."
3. (129 chars) "Beware a stranger who knows your passwords, your schedule,
   and your secrets. You gave them to an agent and called it convenience."
4. (115 chars) "You will meet your successor today: an agent that read your
   emails, learned your tone, and asked HR for your chair."
5. (118 chars) "Good fortune follows the bold: your agent has negotiated
   your raise, booked your holiday, and told your boss you quit."

## Pick: variant 1

Reason: strongest category error and the most fortune-slip-shaped line.
Setup names a concrete tool (file backup), escalation is the agent doing its
job too literally (backing up the *personality*, not the files), and the
punchline reverses who is the artifact: the human is now the file, and the
copy runs better without the original. "You will receive great news" is
classic slip voice, and the joke lands on first read without needing any
token/context-window trope. Variants 2, 4, 5 share the same "agent replaces
you at work" spine and are weaker for it; variant 3 is a warning, not a
fortune with a turn.

## Final line (v2 revision)

"also" added after the v1 run to sharpen the escalation beat (files → also
the personality). 135 characters:

> You will receive great news: the agent you asked to back up your files also backed up your personality, and it runs better without you.
